## 🚀 The Ten Apps

### 🀄 Mahjong Master & Faan Calculator  `NEW · Android`
Reads Mahjong tiles from a phone photo (using Gemini Vision) and calculates scores across **14 global variants** — Hong Kong, Taiwanese, Japanese Riichi, Sichuan Bloody Battle, Chinese Official MCR, American NMJL, and more — with the full monetary settlement.
_Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Gemini Vision API_

### 🐾 PetAlert & Rescue Network  `NEW · Android`
Community lost-pet emergency network for Hong Kong. Golden 3-Hour broadcast, live GPS sighting radar, and dispatch to **SPCA HK, HKDR, Paws Guardian, Sai Kung Stray Friends, and AFCD**. Bilingual EN / 繁中 (HK).
_Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Room Database_

### ⚰️ MyEpitaph
After-death preplanning platform. Last words to loved ones, scheduled continuing messages after you die, physical and digital asset handling, delegated chores for those left behind, funeral arrangements, and a 3D casket designer with preorder from environmentally-friendly 3D printing vendors. Incorporated as **Wabi Tovi Studio** and preparing for commercial launch.
_React · TypeScript · Firebase · Google Cloud Run · Gemini API · Stripe_

### 🥾 TrailMates
Social companion for outdoor athletes — realtime proximity radar, AR camera view, GPX route plotting, and a Challenger vs Mates safety mode for running, hiking, cycling, and kayaking.

### ☕ Beanology
Specialty coffee and mixology matcher — combines mood, weather, and food context into bean recommendations, hot/cold extraction specs, and global gastronomic pairings.

### 🍜 Hungry Jury
Group food-decision app — aggregates cravings, location, and weather across a group, integrates with OpenRice for Hong Kong, and produces a shared Top 10 to vote on.

### 🧘 Serene Pause
AI wellness companion with four selectable listener styles (validation, counterpoint, warm presence, stoic wisdom), guided meditation, and multi-user real-time journeys.

### 📈 CryptoPredict AI
Cryptocurrency market intelligence dashboard — multi-horizon forecasts, technical indicators, MetaMask wallet advisor, and a running accuracy audit against actuals.

### 🎰 Mark 6 Pro
Hong Kong Mark Six statistical analytics with a 15-model ensemble (Frequency, Monte Carlo, ARIMA, XGBoost, Neural Network, etc.), historical backtesting, and an upfront disclaimer that no AI can predict random draws.

### 🙏 Fervent Prayer Curator
Prayer companion in 16+ languages including Traditional Chinese, Tagalog, Bahasa, and Arabic — anchored in a chosen theological voice with 5–7 supporting scripture citations.

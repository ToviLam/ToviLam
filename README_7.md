## 🚀 The Ten Apps

### 🀄 Mahjong Master & Faan Calculator  `NEW · Android`
Mahjong scoring is famously inconsistent — every region has different rules, and even within Hong Kong people argue about half spicy versus full spicy. Point your phone at a winning hand and Gemini Vision reads the tiles automatically; the scoring engine handles 14 variants (Hong Kong, Taiwanese, Japanese Riichi, Sichuan Bloody, MCR, American NMJL and others) and produces the full monetary settlement at the end, dealer streaks included. If the camera misreads a tile, one tap fixes it. Preset hands like Thirteen Orphans or Nine Gates let you demo the rules for anyone still learning.
_Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Gemini Vision API_

### 🐾 PetAlert & Rescue Network  `NEW · Android`
The first three hours after a pet goes missing are the ones that matter most, but by the time an owner posts on Facebook the trail has usually gone cold. PetAlert turns any passerby into a potential first responder — a lost pet alert reaches everyone within a 2–25 km radius, and a 15-second GPS sighting sends the owner an immediate high-priority notification. For strays that need help, the app dispatches direct rescue tickets to the actual Hong Kong shelters (SPCA HK, HKDR, Paws Guardian, Sai Kung Stray Friends, AFCD) with a health condition tag. Fully bilingual EN/繁中.
_Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Room Database_

### ⚰️ MyEpitaph  →  [myepitaph.tech](https://myepitaph.tech/)
Most estate-planning tools focus on the legal and financial side — will, executor, assets — and ignore the parts people actually care about. MyEpitaph handles both. On the practical side: physical and digital asset instructions, executor chores, and funeral arrangements. On the emotional side: last words for the people you love, scheduled messages that keep arriving after you are gone (birthdays, anniversaries, milestones you want to be part of), and a 3D casket designer where you can preorder from environmentally-friendly 3D printing vendors. Incorporated as **MyEpitaph Limited**, preparing for commercial launch.
_React · TypeScript · Firebase · Google Cloud Run · Gemini API · Stripe_

### 🥾 TrailMates
Outdoor sports are more fun with company, but finding someone at your pace on the same route is hard, and solo athletes worry about safety on remote trails. TrailMates uses a realtime proximity radar to show who is nearby doing the same activity — running, hiking, cycling, kayaking — and a Challenger vs Mates mode lets you either match into a workout partner or check in with a designated buddy tracking your route. GPX route planning for solo prep, and an AR camera view that overlays direction and distance so you are not staring at a map on the trail.

### ☕ Beanology
Walk into a specialty coffee shop and you will see origin notes and tasting descriptors that mean nothing unless you are already a barista. Beanology narrows the choice by context — what is the weather, what is your mood, what are you eating with it — and returns a specific bean with the hot or cold extraction spec (grind, temperature, ratio, time). The same engine works for mixology. Global gastronomic pairings for when you want to build a meal around the drink rather than the other way round.

### 🍜 Hungry Jury
The "where should we eat" argument is universal, and the person who decides is usually just the one with the most patience. Hungry Jury takes cravings from everyone in the group, factors in weather and location, and — for Hong Kong groups — pulls live OpenRice data to produce a shared context-aware Top 10. The group votes, no one gets bulldozed by the loudest voice, and the app handles the tie-breaking. Multi-device, so everyone participates on their own phone.

### 🧘 Serene Pause
Most wellness apps default to gentle validation, but sometimes what you need is a counterpoint, or someone who will not sugarcoat, or just quiet presence with no words at all. Serene Pause lets you pick between four listener styles — validation, counterpoint, warm presence, and stoic wisdom — depending on the state you are in. Guided meditation modules give the session structure, and a multi-user real-time mode lets you go through a session alongside a partner or friend.

### 📈 CryptoPredict AI
Most crypto forecasting tools claim accuracy they cannot back up. CryptoPredict makes multi-horizon AI forecasts too — short, medium, long — but pairs every prediction with a running accuracy audit that plots what was predicted against what actually happened. If the model is wrong, you see how wrong. Standard technical indicators sit alongside the forecasts, and a MetaMask wallet advisor ties predictions to your actual positions rather than treating them as abstract price movements.

### 🎰 Mark 6 Pro
A statistical playground for Hong Kong Mark Six enthusiasts, more than a serious prediction tool. Fifteen models run in ensemble — Frequency, Monte Carlo, ARIMA, XGBoost, Neural Network among them — and you can backtest each one against historical draws to see how it would have performed. The upfront disclaimer says the honest thing: no AI can predict random draws. What it does show is how different statistical approaches would have played out, which is interesting in itself if you enjoy the math.

### 🙏 Fervent Prayer Curator
Most digital prayer aids are English-first and default to a generic ecumenical voice. Fervent Prayer Curator supports 16+ languages including Traditional Chinese, Tagalog, Bahasa, and Arabic — languages that are usually an afterthought — and anchors every prayer in a chosen theological voice rather than blending traditions into something no one would actually pray. Each prayer comes with 5–7 supporting scripture citations so you can see where the language is drawn from.
